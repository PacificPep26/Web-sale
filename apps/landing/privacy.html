Consumer Data Protection

Privacy & Data Security Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Regenxlabsbio LLC ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website and brand Regenx Studio. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidential personal information of our clients and visitors in full compliance with United States federal and state regulations.

2. Payment Security & Processing via Stripe

All credit card transactions, digital wallets, and banking payments are processed directly through Stripe, Inc., a certified PCI-DSS Level 1 Service Provider. Regenxlabsbio LLC never captures, logs, or stores unencrypted credit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking credentials on our private servers.

3. Information We Collect

We only collect essential details required to process your transaction and deliver merchandise:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, telephone number.
  • Shipping Information: Physical delivery address and postal code.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, IP address, and session timestamps used for fraud detection.

4. Zero Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers, advertisers, or unaffiliated commercial entities.

5. Data Subject Rights & Contact

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you or request permanent erasure of your order history at any time by contacting our privacy compliance officer at privacy@regenxlabsbio.com.
