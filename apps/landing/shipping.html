Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: September 2026

1. Order Processing Time

All orders placed on Regenx Studio are packaged and dispatched within 1–2 business days (Monday through Friday, excluding standard US federal holidays). Orders placed on weekends or observed holidays are processed on the next business day.

2. Domestic United States Shipping

We provide tracked, insured delivery throughout all 50 states via USPS Priority Mail and DHL eCommerce:

  • Standard Tracked Shipping (5–8 business days): $5.95 USD flat rate (Complimentary on all orders over $75.00 USD).
  • Expedited Priority (2–4 business days): $14.95 USD.

3. Tracking & Confirmation

Once your order leaves our fulfillment center, you will automatically receive a Shipment Confirmation Email containing your unique tracking number and direct tracking link. Tracking information typically activates within 24 hours of package handoff.

4. Packaging Standards

Every eyewear frame is protected by a rigid hardshell leatherette case, anti-scratch microfiber sleeve, and optical cleaning cloth before being secured in an impact-tested shipping box.

5. Lost or Damaged Shipments

If your package arrives damaged or tracking shows no movement for more than 10 consecutive business days, please notify our client care team at support@regenxlabsbio.com with your order number. We will issue an expedited replacement or full refund immediately.
