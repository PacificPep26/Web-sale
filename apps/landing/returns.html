30-Day Money-Back Guarantee & Return Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: September 2026

1. 30-Day Risk-Free Trial

At Regenx Studio, we want you to be completely satisfied with your eyewear and lifestyle accessories. You may return any item in its original condition within 30 days of confirmed delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

2. Return Eligibility & Condition

To qualify for a full refund, returned merchandise must be:

  • Unworn, unaltered, and free of scratches, dents, or signs of wear.
  • Accompanied by all original packaging, protective leather case, microfiber cloth, and warranty tags.

3. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Email our concierge at support@regenxlabsbio.com with your order number and reason for return.
  2. Our support team will generate a prepaid return shipping label and provide detailed packaging instructions within 24 business hours.
  3. Securely pack the item in its original shipping box and drop it off at any authorized USPS carrier location.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Once our Delaware inspection facility receives and inspects the return (usually within 2 business days of delivery), your refund will be initiated immediately. Refunds are returned to your original credit card / payment method processed via Stripe within 3–7 business days depending on your bank.

5. Exchanges & Damaged Items

If an item arrives damaged or you would prefer a different frame model, please notify us immediately. We will rush an exchange shipment with zero additional shipping charges.
